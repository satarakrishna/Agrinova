Fungal Threats

Identify, prevent, and manage fungal diseases to protect crop health.

Common Fungal Diseases

Powdery Mildew

White powdery coating on leaves reducing photosynthesis.

Leaf Rust

Orange/brown spots on leaves leading to reduced yield.

Blight

Rapid leaf browning and plant death due to fungal infection.

Detection & Control Measures

  • Regular leaf inspections for early spots or discoloration.
  • Maintain proper air circulation between plants.
  • Avoid overwatering and waterlogging.
  • Use certified disease-free seeds.
  • Apply fungicides when early symptoms appear.
  • Remove infected plants immediately.
  • Practice crop rotation to break fungal life cycles.
  • Use fungus-resistant crop varieties.
  • Maintain proper plant spacing to improve airflow.
  • Avoid overhead irrigation; use drip irrigation instead.
  • Remove and destroy crop residues after harvest.
