Pest Control

Protecting crops through integrated & eco-friendly practices

Introduction

Pest control involves managing insects, weeds, and diseases that reduce crop yield and quality. Effective pest management ensures food security and sustainable farming.

Types of Pests

  • Insect pests (aphids, caterpillars)
  • Weeds
  • Rodents
  • Plant diseases

Traditional Methods

  • Manual removal
  • Crop rotation
  • Use of natural predators

Chemical Control

  • Insecticides
  • Herbicides
  • Fungicides

Integrated Pest Management (IPM)

IPM combines biological, cultural, mechanical, and chemical methods to control pests while minimizing environmental impact.

Technology Integration

AI-based pest detection, drones, and smart spraying systems help identify infestations early and reduce pesticide usage.

Smart Agriculture System

Integrating technology with agriculture to provide farmers with intelligent crop recommendations, weather insights, and soil analysis for better productivity and sustainable farming.
