Pest & Disease Alerts

Get timely alerts on pests and diseases affecting your crops.

Common Pests & Diseases

Aphids

Small sap-sucking insects that can weaken plants and transmit viruses.

Wheat Rust

Fungal disease causing rust-colored spots on wheat leaves and stems.

Locusts

Swarming insects that can devastate entire crop fields in hours.

Detection & Preventive Measures

  • Regular Field Monitoring – Visual inspections for early signs of pests or diseases.
  • AI-based Pest Detection – Using mobile apps or software to detect pests from leaf images.
  • IoT Sensors & Drones – Monitor crop health and detect abnormalities in real-time.
  • Soil & Leaf Testing – Identify nutrient deficiencies that make plants susceptible.
  • Use Resistant Crop Varieties – Plant pest- and disease-resistant strains.
  • Eco-friendly Pesticides – Use minimal chemical intervention when necessary.
  • Timely Harvesting – Harvest before pests can multiply or spread.
  • Regular Pruning – Remove dead leaves and branches to reduce fungal growth.
