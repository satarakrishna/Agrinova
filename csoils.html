Understanding Soil pH & Nutrient Balance

Healthy soil = healthy crops 🌱

🧪 What is Soil pH?

Soil pH measures acidity or alkalinity. It controls nutrient availability and microbial activity.

  • <6.5 → Acidic soil
  • 6.5–7.5 → Ideal for most crops
  • >7.5 → Alkaline soil

🌾 Why Nutrients Matter

Plants need three primary nutrients for growth:

N → Leaf growth

P → Root & flower development

K → Disease resistance

Ideal Nutrient Levels

Nitrogen 75%

Phosphorus 60%

Potassium 70%

⚠ Effects of Imbalance

  • Low Nitrogen → Yellow leaves
  • Low Phosphorus → Weak roots
  • Low Potassium → Poor resistance

💡 Solutions

  • Add lime to raise pH
  • Add sulfur to lower pH
  • Use organic compost
