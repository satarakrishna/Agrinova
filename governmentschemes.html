Government Schemes

Empowering farmers through financial, technical & social support

Introduction

The Government of India provides multiple schemes to support farmers by offering financial assistance, crop insurance, irrigation facilities, and technological guidance.

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)

Provides ₹6000 per year in three installments to eligible farmers.
🔗 https://pmkisan.gov.in

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

Crop insurance scheme protecting farmers against crop loss due to natural calamities.
🔗 https://pmfby.gov.in

Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchai Yojana (PMKSY)

Focuses on improving irrigation efficiency and water conservation.
🔗 https://pmksy.gov.in

Soil Health Card Scheme

Provides soil nutrient analysis to farmers for better crop planning.
🔗 https://soilhealth.dac.gov.in

e-NAM (National Agriculture Market)

Online trading platform for agricultural commodities across India.
🔗 https://www.enam.gov.in

Digital India & Smart Farming

Government initiatives integrate GIS, AI, IoT, and mobile apps to provide real-time advisory services to farmers.

Smart Agriculture System

Integrating technology with agriculture to provide farmers with intelligent crop recommendations, weather insights, and soil analysis for better productivity and sustainable farming.
