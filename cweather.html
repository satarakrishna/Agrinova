🌧 Using Rainfall Prediction to Reduce Water Waste

Weather forecasting helps farmers plan irrigation efficiently. By knowing upcoming rainfall, farmers can avoid unnecessary watering and conserve groundwater resources.

💧 1. Skip Irrigation Before Rain

If rainfall is predicted, irrigation can be delayed, preventing overwatering and soil nutrient loss.

🌱 2. Maintain Ideal Soil Moisture

Combining rain forecasts with soil moisture data keeps crops in the optimal moisture range.

⚡ 3. Save Energy & Cost

Less pumping of water means lower electricity or diesel expenses for farmers.

🌍 4. Prevent Waterlogging

Knowing heavy rain in advance helps farmers improve drainage and protect crop roots.

🌾 Smart Farming Tip: Use rainfall prediction + drip irrigation for maximum water efficiency.
🌧 Rain Impact

Rain improves soil moisture but excess water may damage roots.

🌡 Temperature Effect

Moderate temperatures support healthy crop growth.

💨 Wind Advisory

Strong winds can cause lodging in tall crops like maize.
