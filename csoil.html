Soil Management

Scientific foundation for sustainable and climate-resilient agriculture

Introduction

Soil management focuses on preserving soil fertility, enhancing biological activity, and ensuring long-term agricultural productivity. Healthy soil is essential for food security and ecosystem balance.

Soil Components

  • Mineral particles (sand, silt, clay)
  • Organic matter
  • Soil water and air
  • Microorganisms

Major Problems

  • Soil erosion
  • Nutrient depletion
  • Salinity and alkalinity
  • Loss of soil biodiversity

Sustainable Practices

  • Crop rotation
  • Organic manure usage
  • Precision irrigation
  • Reduced tillage

Technology Role

GIS mapping, remote sensing, and IoT-based soil sensors enable real-time soil analysis, helping farmers optimize fertilizer usage and improve yields.
